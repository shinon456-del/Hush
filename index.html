Campus study space, reserved in advance

Find your quiet corner before someone else does.

Hush isn't just a library seat map. It pools quiet spaces from across campus — library seats, classrooms sitting empty between classes, courtyard nooks, and other staff-approved rooms — so you always have somewhere to sit, even on a small campus with a small library.

Spaces open today

Every room is rated by how quiet it actually is — not just how it's labelled on a map.

Three steps, no line at the door

1

Search by quiet, not just by room

Filter by zone and noise level so you land in a room that actually matches how you study.

2

Pick a slot and hold your seat

Reserve a two-hour block. Your seat is held for the first 15 minutes if you're running late.

3

Arrive, tap in, concentrate

Show your reservation at the door. No re-booking, no hunting for an outlet — just sit and work.

Quiet hours are the whole point.

Library seats and the Thesis Night Desk are phone-free and conversation-free from open to close. Study areas like The Grove and Student Commons allow low, library-level conversation. Classrooms and other approved spaces released for group work are bookable for discussion at any volume.

"You don't need more coffee. You need forty-five uninterrupted minutes and nobody's group project three seats away."
